# Verification pass: waterfront claims and the change audit (Dartmouth, Kingswear and the lower Dart, June 1967)

Working draft, saved part-way through the pass (19 November 2026). Sections marked "pending" are still being checked.

**What was actually read in full.** Wikipedia articles (wikitext, fetched November 2026); Dartmouth History Research Group (DHRG) timeline page and seven DHRG archive documents (PDF and XLS attachments); the Dart Marina history page; the Cinema Treasures page; the Dart Harbour history page; eleven list entries as reproduced on British Listed Buildings (BLB); and two PDFs on dartmouthplan.org (the Conservation Area Appraisal, PDF dated January 2013, 40 pages; the Devon Historic Coastal and Market Towns Survey, PDF dated July 2021, 65 pages), both converted to text and searched.

**What could not be opened.**
- `historicengland.org.uk`, `www.heritagegateway.org.uk`, `www.francisfrith.com`: each returned HTTP 403 with a "Just a moment... Enable JavaScript and cookies" browser challenge. Not bypassed. Listing texts were read on britishlistedbuildings.co.uk instead (same Historic England text).
- `maps.nls.uk`: every page returned HTTP 405 "Human Verification" (a CAPTCHA). Not bypassed. No map was seen.
- `geo.nls.uk` (an NLS tile host): refused by the session's network policy (proxy answered 403 to CONNECT).
- `mapseries-tilesets.s3.amazonaws.com` (the other NLS tile host): one probe returned an S3 "not found" for a guessed tile path, so the host resolves; a follow-up attempt to read tiles from it was refused by the session's permission system as unauthorised access, and was not pursued.
- `britainfromabove.org.uk`: connection reset three times; `www.britainfromabove.org.uk` refused by network policy.
- The WebFetch tool could not resolve any host in this session; all reads were by curl through the session proxy.

Status words: **confirmed** = the full page says it; **corrected** = the full page says something different; **still unverified** = the cited page could not be opened and no reachable page covers it.

## Which rows of the report's audit tables and waterfront claims are confirmed, which need correcting, and which remain unverified?

### Takeaway
(Draft.) Most dated waterfront claims hold up against full pages, but six need correcting: the gasworks was demolished in the late 1950s (so there is no gasworks in 1967; a "Dartmouth Boatel" bar and club stood there in the 1960s); the bandstand is "late C19" on its listing, not 1911; the station building is "c1864-70" on its listing against Wikipedia's 1889–90; the Lower Ferry tug dates are not on the cited Wikipedia page (they are on Simplon); "marina 1965" is on the DHRG timeline, not the Dart Marina page; and the Tourist Information Centre wrapped round the Newcomen engine house dates from 1993. The six North Embankment apartment blocks still have no build dates, but two of their sites are now pinned to post-1967 by what stood there before.
