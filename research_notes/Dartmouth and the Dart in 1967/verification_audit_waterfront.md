## Which rows of the report's three audit tables are confirmed by full pages, which need correcting, and which remain unverified?

### Takeaway
Most dated waterfront claims hold, and the 1985–90 widening, the 1986 pontoon, the cinema's name, the lifeboat station and *Hindostan* are now confirmed from full pages. Eight things need correcting, the largest being that the gasworks was demolished in the late 1950s (a "Dartmouth Boatel" stood on its site in the 1960s) and that the covered pontoon was the second one, of about 1889, reached since 1928 by a steel bridge; the six North Embankment blocks still have no build dates.

### Inferences
- **North Embankment frontage in 1967.** With the gasworks gone by about 1960 and flats arriving only in the 1980s, the strip from King's Quay to Coronation Park was low and mixed: the Boatel, the former paint works, the St John Ambulance station and a clinic. - **Kingswear lighthouse.** Dart Harbour's notice describes two sector lights at one station, "the main sector light" and "the old sector light", at the charted position. ### Inferences
- The report's conclusion that further building removals buy little needs one amendment: College Way is a whole road, visible from the air and from the river, and it postdates the game by two years.
- The tankers "bringing fuel to Sandquay for the boilers" were supplying the Naval College, so a small coastal tanker alongside at Sandquay is a sourced 1967 sight; the pilot does not name the berth.
- The Steamboat Company's office at 8 South Embankment gives the first sourced place for its signage in 1967, next door to the Post Office at No. 9.

### Gaps
- DHRG's directories are working files "still under development" with many undated rows, compiled from directories, newspapers and memory. Treat single rows as medium confidence.
- I did not read the picture volumes of the DHRG's Cyril King archive, which may show Noss and the river in the 1960s, or the book *Dartmouth Then – Dartmouth Now*, which is not online.
- No source was found for which named North Embankment block replaced the Boatel, the clinic or the ambulance station.
